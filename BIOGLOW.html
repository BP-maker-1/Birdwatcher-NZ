Aotearoa Biodiversity & Conservation

Protecting native birds with smart trail cameras.

New Zealand’s native bird species face constant threats from introduced predators and habitat loss. Our FLL BIOGLOW Innovation Project uses our own trained AI to automatically capture and sort native bird sightings from backyard and sanctuary trail cameras, helping conservationists track population recovery without disturbing wildlife.

How Our Research Works

Connecting local communities, trail cameras, and conservation groups across New Zealand.

Capture local Birds

02

Automatic AI Species Detection

Our in house trained AI model identifies 400 different species of New Zealand native birds with 90% accuracy.

03

Shared Conservation Log

We share our data and work with conservationists, the University of Otago and Orokonui sancturaries.


Our specialised AI trained in house recognises any species of birds found in New Zealand. We trained it on 100 classes of 400 birds each and 1 contingency for false positives, which contains 1,200 pictures of rocks, plants, and insects, which we sourced from creative comman lisence images from iNaturalist. Our AI model recognises the species, outlines it and gives a percentage on how confident that it is correct. Our model has been tested to over 90% accuracy over many trials. We use trail camera footage in seperate locations to track birds, taking the manual labour out of manually tracking, the only solution at the moment. We found that the AI works best when the bird is relatively unobscured and large in the frame. It gets less reliable when this is not true.
